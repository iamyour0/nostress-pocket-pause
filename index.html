A small moment, just for you

Let a leaf drift around the pond. Sometimes it finds a quiet nook along the shore and gets caught. A little water ripple can help it find its way again.

Little pond visitors

Tap the water three times to invite a little fish. Eight pebbles may attract a bigger fish. The little fish wanders freely, while the bigger fish follows it and must go around pebbles. Leaves never block either fish. Four pebbles invite a frog. 🍃
